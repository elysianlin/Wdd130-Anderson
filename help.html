Ranking Zone

A place for the greatest movies ever made.

1

Cars

"Cars" (2006) follows Lightning McQueen, a rookie race car who learns that life is about more than winning. After getting stranded in a forgotten town, he discovers friendship, humility, and the value of slowing down.

2

The Lion King

Disney’s The Lion King (1994) is a coming-of-age tale about Simba, a young lion prince who must reclaim his rightful place as king after tragedy and betrayal. It explores themes of identity, responsibility, and the “circle of life” in a richly animated African savanna.

3

Kung Fu Panda

"Kung Fu Panda" (2008) is a heartwarming martial arts comedy about Po, a clumsy panda who defies expectations to become a kung fu hero. Chosen by fate, he must overcome self-doubt and train with legendary warriors to protect his homeland from a powerful enemy.

4

The Incredibles

"The Incredibles" (2004) is a thrilling animated adventure about a family of undercover superheroes who must come out of retirement to save the world. It blends action, humor, and heart as they rediscover the power of teamwork and identity.

5

Hercules

Disney’s Hercules (1997) is a myth-inspired animated adventure about a demigod who must prove himself a true hero to reclaim his place among the gods. It’s a story of strength, identity, and self-sacrifice, told with humor, heart, and gospel-style music.

6

Aladdin

Disney’s Aladdin (1992) is a magical adventure about a street-smart young man who discovers a genie-filled lamp and uses its power to win the heart of a princess and stop an evil sorcerer. It’s a tale of identity, freedom, and courage to be yourself.

7

Spider-Man Across the Spider verse

"Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse" (2023) is a visually stunning sequel that follows Miles Morales as he journeys through the multiverse, confronting new threats and challenging what it means to be a hero. It deepens the emotional stakes and expands the Spider-Verse with dazzling animation and complex character arcs.

8

Peter Pan

Disney’s Peter Pan (1953) is a whimsical adventure about a boy who refuses to grow up and takes three children on a magical journey to Neverland. It explores themes of childhood, imagination, and the bittersweet nature of growing up.

9

Mulan

Disney’s Mulan (1998) is an empowering tale of courage and identity, where a young woman disguises herself as a man to save her father and ends up becoming one of China’s greatest heroes. It blends action, humor, and heartfelt themes of honor and self-discovery.

10

The Emperor’s New Groove

Disney’s The Emperor’s New Groove (2000) is a fast-paced comedy about a selfish emperor who’s turned into a llama and must learn humility with the help of a kind-hearted peasant. It’s a buddy adventure filled with slapstick humor, quirky characters, and a surprising amount of heart.
